The company

One accountable partner from requirement to in-service support.

Founded in 1983, Semenanjung Selatan Sdn Bhd has grown into a global trader and logistics partner for armed forces, government agencies and specialist marine operators.

Serving airborne, army, naval and special forces requirements, we combine OEM representation with a sole-source supply channel for government agencies. That means an accredited route to the right equipment — and a team that stays present after delivery.

How we work

A clear route from requirement to readiness.

Procurement is a process, not a transaction. Every enquiry is handled with the same discipline: understand the operating requirement, specify the right system, supply through the correct channel, then support what we deliver.

01 / Define

Clarify the requirement

We start with mission profile, operating environment and the outcome the team needs to achieve.

02 / Specify

Select the system

We match requirements to proven OEM products, configurations and supporting equipment.

03 / Supply

Manage the route

Procurement and logistics are handled through the accredited channel with clear accountability.

04 / Sustain

Support in service

Spares, repair and technical support help protect availability over the long term.
