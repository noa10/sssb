SSS / Diving systems
Diving equipment

Life support for the full mission profile.

We provide an extensive range of general diving and life-support equipment specifically developed for the varied requirements and operational demands of defence divers.

Defence diving spans multiple disciplines. Each one demands mission-specific equipment, supporting systems and training. Our range covers the operational core: breathing systems, navigation, propulsion, recompression and the modular gear divers configure around the task.
