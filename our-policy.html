Quality management

Built to recognised defence standards.

The quality management system of SSS complies with NATO design, development and production requirements. The certificate is issued by the Defence Standardization, Codification and Government Quality Assurance Authority and is based on the ISO 9001:2008 standard, with a number of additions and specifications.
