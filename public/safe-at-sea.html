Safe at Sea

Safe at Sea is a Swedish manufacturer of compact maritime search-and-rescue systems. Its equipment is developed for rescue organisations and professional operators working on coastal and inland waters.

The company’s portfolio includes RescueRunner and other specialist craft, as well as launch and recovery solutions, personal equipment and training. SSSB can help you explore the options for your organisation.
