Diving Equipment / Poland

Seacraft

Seacraft is the underwater propulsion and navigation brand of Marine Tech S.A., based in Krosno, Poland. The company designs and manufactures diver propulsion vehicles and electronic navigation consoles.

Its portfolio includes recreational and technical diving scooters alongside military and special-purpose systems. Explore the individual products below, then speak with SSSB about the configuration you need.
