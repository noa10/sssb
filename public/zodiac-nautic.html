Zodiac Nautic

Zodiac Nautic is a French inflatable boat and rigid inflatable boat manufacturer. Its portfolio covers compact tenders, open-deck RIBs and larger leisure craft for a range of activities on the water.

The range includes OPEN, PRO and MEDLINE boats alongside tenders and yacht dinghies. Explore the individual models below, or contact SSSB to discuss your intended use and equipment requirements.
