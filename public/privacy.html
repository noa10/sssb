Privacy

Your information, plainly explained.

What we collect when you use this website, chat with Haji or request a quotation, and what happens to it afterwards.

Last updated 4 November 2026
At a glance
  • Chat messages are available for up to 30 days after a chat starts.
  • Account details and quotation requests can remain after chat messages are removed.
  • Cookies support sign-in, security and chat sessions.
  • Messages may be processed by an AI service provider.
Ask a privacy question

Who we are

Semenanjung Selatan Sdn Bhd is a Malaysian supplier of diving equipment, parachute systems and inflatable systems. Our head office is at No 1 Jalan Lurah Utama, Kempas Baru, 81200 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia.

This page describes information used by this website, the Haji assistant and the connected account and quotation features. For privacy questions, contact info@semenanjungselatan.com or call +60 7-235 2330.

What we collect

Information submitted through the service includes:

  • Account details. Your email address, full name, phone number, company and password for signing in.
  • Chat content. The messages you send to Haji, the assistant’s replies, timestamps and whether the chat is active or closed.
  • Quotation requests. The product or enquiry you select, quantity, required-by date, delivery location, procurement route and any supporting details you enter, together with the contact details on your account.
  • Technical information. Session cookies and identifiers used for sign-in, security and chat sessions. The service also uses IP-based rate limits to protect against misuse.

How the service uses it

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To answer questions about our company, products and services.
  • To make quotation requests and contact details available to our team for follow-up.
  • To protect the service against misuse.

The Haji assistant

Haji is an AI assistant for Semenanjung Selatan. Messages you send may be processed by an AI service provider so that a reply can be generated, and replies are grounded in approved product and company information.

An AI assistant can make mistakes. Please confirm specifications, availability and pricing with our team before relying on them. Please do not share sensitive, confidential or classified information in the chat.

How long information is kept

Chat messages are no longer available in your account or to staff 30 days after the chat starts. A scheduled cleanup deletes those messages after that point, although the conversation record itself may remain.

Account details and quotation requests can remain after chat messages are removed. Contact our team for information about their retention.

Cookies and sessions

The service uses cookies to keep you signed in, protect forms against cross-site request forgery and maintain chat sessions.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser, but signing in and chatting may stop working if you do.

Who can access information

Our staff can access conversations and quotation requests through the service. Depending on the service configuration, chat messages may also be sent to an AI provider to generate replies.

For more information about how personal data is handled or shared, contact our team.

Privacy questions and requests

You can contact us to ask about access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data. Email info@semenanjungselatan.com or call +60 7-235 2330.
