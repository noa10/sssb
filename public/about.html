Who we are

Specialist supply, built around operational reality.

Semenanjung Selatan Sdn Bhd is a leading Malaysian supplier of military equipment, specialising in airborne, army, naval and special forces requirements.

Our product portfolio is organised into three categories: Diving Equipment, Parachute Systems and Inflatable Systems.

Explore rebreathers from JFD, diver propulsion and navigation from Seacraft, parachutes and activation devices from FXC, inflatable boats and RIBs from Zodiac Nautic, and rescue craft from Safe at Sea. Each company page brings together its introduction and individual products with specifications and key features.

We are the exclusive Malaysian importer and company representative for the specific brands we promote, operating under commercial agreements with original equipment manufacturers. All agency-level government procurement is handled directly by SSSB as the supplier.

Technological innovation remains a cornerstone of our strategic plan. We continue to research new technology so we can meet future requirements with relevant, sustainable system solutions.

Direction & discipline

What guides the work.

Our vision, mission and operating values are deliberately practical. They describe how we make decisions, measure quality and remain accountable to the people who rely on what we supply.

Our vision

To contribute to the world’s progress and safety through meaningful, innovative technological solutions — supported by an accountable supply chain and a high standard of customer service.

Our mission

To continually improve the effectiveness of our quality management system and provide products and services that comply with, and aim to exceed, client expectations.

Our commitment

Professional, passionate service, dependable after-sales support and a focus on product excellence, functionality and delivery requirements.
