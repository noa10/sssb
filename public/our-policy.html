Quality management

Built to recognised defence standards.

The quality management system of SSSB complies with NATO design, development and production requirements. The certificate is issued by the Defence Standardization, Codification and Government Quality Assurance Authority and is based on the ISO 9001:2008 standard, with a number of additions and specifications.

Moving forward

Improvement is a year-on-year discipline.

A fundamental part of our business operating standard is continual improvement. We challenge ourselves to make improvements year to year — in the products we source, the partners we work with and the support we provide after delivery.
